# Card payment dispute

Applies when the payment provider notifies a dispute (chargeback) on an order.

## Answer within the deadline

A dispute must be answered within 7 days. Gather the invoice, the proof of delivery and every message exchanged with the customer.

## Do not refund a disputed payment

While a dispute is open, never refund the payment from the back office: the customer would be paid twice. Wait for the provider's decision.

## Contact the customer once

Write to the customer once, politely: many disputes are a statement line they did not recognise. If they confirm the purchase, ask them to withdraw the dispute with their bank.
