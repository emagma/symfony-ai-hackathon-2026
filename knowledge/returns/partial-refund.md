# Partial refund

Applies when part of an order is refunded: one item of several, or a commercial gesture.

## What can be refunded partially

A returned item is refunded at the price paid for it, promotions deducted. Shipping is refunded only when the whole order comes back or the shop is at fault.

## Commercial gesture

A gesture of up to 10% of the order total can be decided by any member of the team. Above that, ask the team lead. Always write the reason on the order.

## Never refund twice

Before refunding, check the payments of the order: a refund already issued by the payment provider after a dispute must not be issued again from the back office.
