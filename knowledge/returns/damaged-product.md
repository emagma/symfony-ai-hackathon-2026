# Damaged product returned

Applies when an item sent back by a customer arrives damaged, broken or defective at the warehouse.

## Check the item on receipt

Photograph the item and its parcel before opening anything else, and attach the photos to the order. Note whether the outer parcel is damaged too: it tells a transport damage from a damage in use.

## Decide who bears the damage

If the parcel is damaged, or the customer reported the defect within 14 days of delivery, the shop bears it. If the item shows clear signs of use (worn, washed, stained) and the parcel is intact, the return is refused and the item is sent back to the customer at our cost.

## Refund or exchange

When the shop bears the damage, refund the order line in full, shipping included, within 5 working days. Offer an exchange first when the same size is in stock; never offer a voucher instead of a refund unless the customer asks for one.

## Tell the customer

Write to the customer the same day, with the decision and the delay. Never ask them to send photos again if we already have ours.
