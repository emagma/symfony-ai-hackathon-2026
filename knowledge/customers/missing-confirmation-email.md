# Customer did not receive the order confirmation

Applies when a customer says they never received the confirmation email of an order.

## Check the order exists

Find the order by its number or the customer's email. If there is no order, the payment did not go through: say so, and never take a payment detail by email or phone.

## Check the address

A typing mistake in the email address is the usual cause. Correct the customer's email only after they confirm the right one from another channel.

## Send the confirmation again

Resend the order confirmation email from the order. Tell the customer to check their spam folder, and that the email comes from the shop's own address.
