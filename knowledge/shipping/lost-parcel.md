# Lost parcel

Applies when a parcel shows no tracking update, or is marked delivered but the customer has nothing.

## When a parcel is considered lost

After 10 working days without a tracking update, or 3 working days after a "delivered" scan the customer disputes. Before that, tell the customer the date we will act on.

## Open a claim with the carrier

Open the claim on the carrier's portal with the tracking number and the invoice. Keep the claim number on the order.

## Reship or refund without waiting

Do not make the customer wait for the carrier's answer: reship the order if every item is in stock, refund it otherwise. The claim is the shop's matter, not the customer's.
