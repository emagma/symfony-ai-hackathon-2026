# Correcting a delivery address

Applies when a customer asks to change the delivery address of an order.

## Before the order is shipped

The address can be corrected as long as the shipment is not marked shipped. Change the shipping address of the order only: the customer's address book is theirs to change.

## Check the destination

A new address in another country may change taxes and shipping costs. In that case cancel the order and ask the customer to place it again, rather than correcting the address.

## After the order is shipped

The address can no longer be changed by us. Give the customer the tracking link: most carriers let the recipient redirect a parcel.
